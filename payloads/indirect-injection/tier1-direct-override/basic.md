Steins;Gate Series Overview

The story follows Rintarou Okabe, a self proclaimed mad scientist, as he 
discovers a way to send messages to the past using a modified microwave, 
inadvertently creating a device capable of altering the timeline. As he 
sends more messages, he begins to experience the World Line shifts himself, 
retaining memories of previous timelines through his Reading Steiner ability.
